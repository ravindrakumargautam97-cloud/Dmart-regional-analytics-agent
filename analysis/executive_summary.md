

\# DMart Regional Director - Executive Summary



\*\*Dataset:\*\* Synthetic DMart regional store data

\*\*Period:\*\* 1 April - 29 June 2026

\*\*Author:\*\* Ravindra Kumar Gautam

\*\*Scope:\*\* 450 cleaned daily records, 5 stores



\## Headline



Pune is the priority store for revenue improvement, while Hyderabad requires an inventory availability review.



\## Magnitude



Pune's average daily revenue was INR 636,358.55, 26.14% below the regional average of INR 861,535.24. Pune achieved only 70.71% of its daily revenue target.



\## Evidence and Interpretation



\- Pune's average footfall was 1,517.71, approximately 25% below the regional average.

\- Pune's revenue per footfall was INR 419.29, only 1.52% below the regional average.

\- Pune ranked fourth of five stores in average stockout hours (2.32).

\- Hyderabad had the highest average stockout hours (3.85).

\- Mumbai led average daily revenue at INR 1,064,371.60.



Lower footfall is associated with Pune's revenue gap. Stockout ranking does not support the claim that Pune has the most stockouts. These descriptive findings do not establish causation.



\## Unconfirmed Hypotheses



1\. Local demand or accessibility may reduce Pune's footfall. Verify using catchment, competitor and customer-visit data.

2\. Product assortment or pricing may affect conversion. Verify using transaction-level sales, SKU availability and competitor prices.



\## Actions and Timeline



\- \*\*Pune Store Manager:\*\* Review footfall trends and local demand within 7 days.

\- \*\*Hyderabad Inventory Manager:\*\* Audit replenishment and stockout patterns within 7 days.

\- \*\*Regional Director:\*\* Review findings and approve a 30-day improvement plan after validating operational causes.
