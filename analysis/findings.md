## 3. Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Mumbai Leads Revenue Performance

Mumbai recorded the highest average daily revenue of INR 1,064,371.60 and the highest average target achievement of 101.37%.

### Finding 2 — Pune Requires Priority Attention

Pune recorded the lowest average daily revenue of INR 636,358.55 and the lowest average target achievement of 70.71%.

Pune's average daily revenue is approximately 26.14% below the all-store average.

Pune's average footfall of 1,517.71 is approximately 25.00% below the all-store average.

### Finding 3 — Hyderabad Has the Highest Stockout Hours

Hyderabad recorded 3.85 average daily stockout hours, compared with the all-store average of 2.74 hours.

### Finding 4 — Bengaluru Performs Close to Target

Bengaluru achieved an average of 98.01% of its daily target, ranking second among the five stores by average target achievement.

## 4. Recommended Actions

1. Prioritize investigation of Pune's lower footfall and revenue performance.
2. Review Hyderabad's inventory availability and replenishment processes.
3. Study Mumbai's operating practices for potential improvements at other stores.
4. Monitor store-level revenue, footfall, target achievement and stockout hours regularly.
5. Validate operational causes before making decisions; these descriptive results do not establish causation.

## 5. Conclusion

Mumbai leads overall revenue performance, while Pune requires the most attention for revenue and target achievement. Hyderabad has the highest average stockout hours. Further investigation is required before attributing performance differences to specific causes.
